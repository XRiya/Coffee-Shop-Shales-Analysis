# Coffee-Shop-Shales-Analysis
Performed an analysis on coffee shop sales data to gain insights into sales trends and performance. Using Excel, I created a dashboard featuring below key graphs:

# Overall performance:

· Total Transactions: 2,92,224

· Total revenue: $13,25,987

# Weekly Events:

· Monday is the busiest day with 21,643 transactions

· Saturday was the slowest day with 20,510 transactions

· Consistent customers throughout the week, and slightly down on the weekends

# Monthly Income Growth:

· Strong increase from January to June.

· June shows record receipts of $166,485.88.

· There was a significant increase in revenue from March ($98,834.68) to April ($1,18,941.08).

# Production performance:

· Coffee is the leading sector with 58,416 jobs.

· Tia is the second most popular with 45,449 items.

· The bakery sector accounts for 22,706 jobs.

# Best sellers:

· Brewed Chai tea tops the list with 17,183 jobs and $77,081.95 in revenue.

· After gourmet produced coffee, 16,912 jobs generated $70,034.60.

· Barista Espresso ranks third with 16,403 transactions but the highest revenue of $91,406.20.

# Storage areas:

· Three locations: Astoria, Hell’s Kitchen, and Lower Manhattan.

· Data identifies multiple areas that contribute to overall performance.

# Miscellaneous:

· Extensive offerings from beverages (coffee, tea, chocolate) to food (bakery, biscotti) and even clothing.
